Why Atomi

A POS built for how you actually trade

Most point-of-sale systems assume a fast connection, a card terminal and one kind of shop. Atomi starts from the reality of a Kenyan SME: M-Pesa is the default, the network is unreliable, and KRA eTIMS is part of every sale.

The average business still runs on a notebook, a calculator and trust. Cash, M-Pesa and card are reconciled by hand, and leaks only surface at stocktake.

Keeps selling offline

Power cut, dead zone or crowded market. The till keeps selling. Every sale lands in a local outbox first, then syncs once with idempotent retries.

M-Pesa, properly

A real STK push, not a “mark as paid” button. The sale stays pending until Safaricom confirms the exact amount; missed callbacks are reconciled automatically.

eTIMS on every sale

Compliance is a checkout step, not another device. Every sale and credit note clears with KRA, and the receipt carries the clearance number and QR code.
