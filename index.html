Why Atomi

A POS built for how you actually trade

Most point-of-sale systems assume a fast connection, a card terminal and one kind of shop. Atomi starts from the reality of a Kenyan SME: M-Pesa is the default, the network is unreliable, and KRA eTIMS is part of every sale.

Keeps selling offline

Sales are saved locally first, then synced in the background. Idempotent retries mean a sale is never applied twice.

M-Pesa, properly

A real STK push — the sale finalises only when Safaricom confirms the exact amount. Missed callbacks are reconciled automatically.

eTIMS on every sale

Invoices and credit notes clear with KRA. The receipt carries the clearance number and QR code — no bolt-on device.
